# Articles

### Charts in R

- [Price charts in
  R](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemcrypto/articles/golemcrypto.md):

  Download cryptocurrency prices in R and draw interactive candlestick
  charts, compare coins and see their monthly returns.

- [Volatility, drawdowns and
  seasonality](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemcrypto/articles/analyses.md):

  The volatility, drawdowns and seasonality of a cryptocurrency in R,
  with a GARCH forecast of its volatility.

- [The top 100
  coins](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemcrypto/articles/market.md):

  The 100 cryptocurrencies with the largest market cap in R, as a
  treemap and a table, from the CoinMarketCap API.
